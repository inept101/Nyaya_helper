PLAINT UNDER ORDER VII RULE 1 OF THE CODE OF CIVIL PROCEDURE, 1908,
READ WITH SECTION 9 OF THE CODE OF CIVIL PROCEDURE, 1908.


MOST RESPECTFULLY SHOWETH:

1. That the Plaintiff is the elder son of Late Sh. Mohan Lal Sharma, who
   expired intestate on 14th March, 2023, at New Delhi. The Plaintiff and
   Defendant No. 1 are the only sons, and Defendant No. 2 is the widow, of
   the deceased Sh. Mohan Lal Sharma. Each of them is therefore a Class-I
   legal heir of the deceased under Section 8 read with the Schedule of the
   Hindu Succession Act, 1956, and entitled to an equal one-third share in
   the estate of the deceased.

2. That during his lifetime, the deceased Sh. Mohan Lal Sharma was the
   absolute owner of the following immovable properties (hereinafter
   collectively referred to as the "Suit Properties"):

   (a) PROPERTY A: Residential House No. 1247, Gali No. 6, Karol Bagh,
       New Delhi - 110005, measuring approximately 250 square yards,
       comprising of ground floor, first floor, and a barsati on the second
       floor, purchased by the deceased vide registered Sale Deed dated
       17th August, 1981, registered as document No. 4421 in Book No. I,
       Volume No. 312, at pages 88-95, in the office of the Sub-Registrar,
       Karol Bagh, Delhi.

   (b) PROPERTY B: Shop No. 14, Ground Floor, Gaffar Market, Karol Bagh,
       New Delhi - 110005, measuring 12 ft x 18 ft (216 sq. ft.), purchased
       vide registered Sale Deed dated 23rd November, 1994, registered as
       document No. 8814 in Book No. I, in the office of the Sub-Registrar,
       Karol Bagh, Delhi. The said shop is currently let out to one M/s
       Khanna Electronics on a monthly rent of Rs. 45,000/-.

   (c) PROPERTY C: Agricultural land bearing Khasra No. 287/2, measuring
       2 bighas and 4 biswas, situated in Village Mehrampur, Tehsil
       Sonepat, District Sonepat, Haryana, acquired by the deceased vide
       registered Sale Deed dated 9th February, 2002.

3. That the deceased Sh. Mohan Lal Sharma died intestate. No Will, codicil,
   or testamentary document has been left behind by him. The Plaintiff has
   made diligent enquiries with all family members, the family lawyer one
   Sh. Vinod Aggarwal, Advocate, and at the residence of the deceased; no
   Will or document of testamentary disposition has been discovered.

4. That immediately upon the death of the deceased, the Plaintiff,
   Defendant No. 1, and Defendant No. 2 came into joint possession of the
   Suit Properties as co-owners and as legal heirs in equal shares of
   one-third each.

5. That with effect from the month of June, 2023, Defendant No. 1, in
   collusion with Defendant No. 2, began to exclude the Plaintiff from the
   peaceful enjoyment of Property A. The first floor of Property A, which
   was historically occupied by the Plaintiff and his family, was forcibly
   locked by Defendant No. 1 on or about 10th June, 2023, while the
   Plaintiff and his family were away at Haridwar for the immersion of the
   ashes of the deceased. Upon returning, the Plaintiff was denied entry
   to the said first floor. The locks were changed without notice to or
   consent of the Plaintiff.

6. That further, Defendant No. 1 has been collecting the entire monthly
   rent of Rs. 45,000/- from M/s Khanna Electronics in respect of Property
   B from June, 2023 onwards, and has not paid the Plaintiff his rightful
   one-third share (Rs. 15,000/- per month) despite repeated demands made
   orally and in writing. As on the date of institution of this suit
   (October, 2025), arrears of mesne profits in respect of Property B
   alone amount to Rs. 4,20,000/- (Rupees Four Lakh Twenty Thousand only)
   for 28 months.

7. That in respect of Property C (the agricultural land at Mehrampur),
   the Defendant No. 1 has, without consent of the Plaintiff, leased out
   the said land for cultivation to one Sh. Dharampal Singh, S/o Sh. Hukam
   Singh, R/o Village Mehrampur, on a yearly lease of Rs. 80,000/-, and
   has appropriated the entire lease amount to himself.

8. That on 5th August, 2023, the Plaintiff, through his counsel Sh.
   Manish Khanna, Advocate, issued a legal notice under Section 80 of the
   Code of Civil Procedure (sent by registered post AD on 5th August,
   2023, delivered on 8th August, 2023) calling upon the Defendants to:
   (i) effect partition of the Suit Properties by metes and bounds in
   equal one-third shares, (ii) render true and faithful accounts of all
   rents and lease amounts received since June, 2023, and (iii) pay over
   to the Plaintiff his one-third share thereof. The Defendants neither
   replied to the said notice nor complied with the demands. The notice
   was served on both Defendants on 8th August, 2023, as evidenced by the
   postal AD receipt, copy of which is filed herewith.

9. That on 18th September, 2025, when the Plaintiff visited Property A
   for the Shradh ceremony of the deceased, Defendant No. 1 along with
   four unknown persons obstructed the Plaintiff from entering the
   premises, threatened him with physical harm, and stated that the
   Plaintiff "shall not see this house again." An FIR was registered at
   Police Station Karol Bagh on the same day vide FIR No. 0892/2025
   under Sections 506 and 341 of the Indian Penal Code, 1860. The
   Plaintiff is constrained to apprehend that the Defendants may, in the
   interregnum, alienate, encumber, mortgage, or part with possession of
   the Suit Properties to defeat the just claims of the Plaintiff.

10. That the Plaintiff has not been able to amicably resolve the dispute
    despite the intervention of several family elders, including the
    Plaintiff's maternal uncle Sh. Krishan Lal Aggarwal and one Sh. Om
    Prakash Sharma, neighbour. All attempts at amicable partition have
    failed on account of the obstinate and avaricious conduct of the
    Defendants.

11. That the cause of action for the present suit arose firstly on
    14th March, 2023, upon the death of the deceased Sh. Mohan Lal Sharma;
    secondly, in June, 2023, when Defendant No. 1 commenced exclusion of
    the Plaintiff from Property A and began appropriating the rents of
    Property B; thirdly, on 8th August, 2023, when the legal notice
    remained unanswered; and finally and continuously, on 18th September,
    2025, when the Defendants threatened the Plaintiff and obstructed his
    access. The cause of action is continuing in nature.

12. That the Suit Properties are situated within the territorial
    jurisdiction of this Hon'ble Court [Properties A and B], and as
    regards Property C situated at Sonepat, Haryana, the parties have by
    consent and convenience agreed that the entire suit may be tried by
    this Hon'ble Court under Section 17 of the Code of Civil Procedure,
    1908. This Hon'ble Court has pecuniary and territorial jurisdiction
    to try and entertain the present suit.

13. That for the purposes of court fees and jurisdiction, the value of
    the Suit is fixed at Rs. 5,75,00,000/- (Rupees Five Crore Seventy Five
    Lakh only), being the approximate market value of the Suit Properties,
    calculated as follows: Property A — Rs. 4,50,00,000/-; Property B —
    Rs. 85,00,000/-; Property C — Rs. 40,00,000/-. Ad valorem court fee
    on the one-third share claimed by the Plaintiff is being affixed
    hereto, in accordance with the Court Fees Act, 1870 read with the
    Delhi High Court (Original Side) Rules.

14. That the Plaintiff is filing the present suit for partition of the
    Suit Properties, possession of his one-third share therein, permanent
    injunction restraining the Defendants from alienating, parting with
    possession of, or creating any third-party interests in the Suit
    Properties, and for mesne profits in respect of rents and lease
    amounts wrongfully appropriated by the Defendants.


PRAYER

It is therefore most respectfully prayed that this Hon'ble Court may
graciously be pleased to:

(a) Pass a preliminary decree declaring that the Plaintiff is entitled
    to an undivided one-third share in each of the Suit Properties
    described in paragraph 2 above, and directing partition of the same
    by metes and bounds;

(b) Pass a final decree of partition through the Court's Local
    Commissioner appointed for the purpose, and put the Plaintiff in
    separate and exclusive possession of his one-third share so allotted;

(c) Grant a decree of permanent injunction restraining the Defendants,
    their servants, agents, representatives, and any person claiming
    through them, from alienating, transferring, mortgaging, leasing,
    encumbering, or creating any third-party interests in the Suit
    Properties during the pendency of the suit and thereafter;

(d) Direct the Defendants to render true and faithful accounts of all
    rents, lease amounts, and any other income received in respect of
    Properties B and C from 14th March, 2023, till the date of decree,
    and to pay over to the Plaintiff his one-third share thereof,
    together with interest at the rate of 12% per annum;

(e) Pass a decree for mesne profits in favour of the Plaintiff against
    Defendant No. 1 in the sum of Rs. 4,20,000/- (in respect of Property
    B, calculated up to October, 2025) and Rs. 53,333/- (in respect of
    Property C, being one-third of two years' lease), together with
    further mesne profits till the date of separate possession;

(f) Award costs of the suit to the Plaintiff;

(g) Pass such other and further orders as this Hon'ble Court may deem
    just, fit, and proper in the facts and circumstances of the case.
